IRT theta-phi harmonic investigation
=====================================

Data sample
-----------
Input: filtered.recodata.fingers.root
Selection: exactly one accepted ring per frame
Statistics: 10000 frames, 144435 Cherenkov hits
Objective: robust residual of reconstructed theta minus target theta
Target theta: 0.037921467 rad

Baseline geometry
-----------------
track eta = 3.09673
track phi = 0.262884
mirror shift = (+2.11642, -12.6748, 0) mm
detector centre shift = (+0.197618, +0.00373014, 0) mm

Baseline theta-versus-phi harmonics
-----------------------------------
A0  = -0.000451482
Ac1 = -0.000807094
As1 = -0.000505082
Ac2 =  0.000903788
As2 =  0.00189909

Focused track fit
------------------
Fitting only track eta and track phi, with all geometry fixed, gave:
track eta = 3.09398 +/- 0.00033
track phi = 0.262245 +/- 0.00018

The fitted-track harmonics were:
A0  = -0.000417149
Ac1 = -0.000855910
As1 = -0.000379160
Ac2 =  0.000901842
As2 =  0.00192554

Conclusion: the global track direction does not remove the modulation. The
harmonic coefficients are essentially unchanged, especially the dominant
second harmonic.

Interpretation
--------------
The strongest remaining responses are detector_theta and detector_tilt_x,
followed by track_phi, track_eta, and detector centre x. Detector y, detector
phi, mirror y, and the longitudinal coordinates are much less influential in
this objective. The apparent emission_z preference at the scan boundary is
not evidence of a reliable measurement; it indicates weak or monotonic
sensitivity over the tested interval.

Next diagnostic
---------------
Fit detector_theta and detector_tilt_x together while keeping track, mirror,
and detector centre fixed. Then inspect the harmonics again. If the second
harmonic remains, test detector_theta with detector centre x and detector
tilt_y separately, followed by a controlled two-parameter fit. Do not release
all parameters simultaneously until these correlations are understood.

Focused detector fit
---------------------
The fit of detector_theta and detector_tilt_x on the same 10000-frame sample
gave:
detector_theta = -0.648041 +/- 0.001601 rad
detector_tilt_x = 1.34337e-05 +/- 0.001599 rad

The fitted harmonics were:
A0  = -0.000455810
Ac1 = -0.000801748
As1 = -0.000526943
Ac2 =  0.000903656
As2 =  0.00189437

Conclusion: this pair does not improve the modulation. The fitted theta is
effectively the nominal value and the tilt is consistent with zero. The
dominant second harmonic is unchanged. Detector_theta is therefore sensitive
in the scalar theta objective but is not the source of the residual
theta-versus-phi pattern.

Transverse four-parameter fit
------------------------------
Fitting mirror x/y and detector centre x/y together gave:
mirror = (-86.41, -60.93) mm relative to the baseline fit
detector = (-100.00, -53.28) mm relative to the baseline fit
The detector x parameter reached its lower limit. The scalar objective was
lower, but the solution is clearly unphysical and is not a valid geometry
interpretation.

The harmonics at that boundary solution were:
A0  =  0.000874
Ac1 = -0.001616
As1 = -0.001430
Ac2 =  0.000700
As2 =  0.001714

Conclusion: the scalar theta objective can be reduced by exploiting broad
transverse geometry degeneracies, but this does not remove the harmonic
pattern and drives parameters to unphysical boundaries. The harmonic pattern
must therefore be fitted directly, or the remaining issue is in the detector
hit-coordinate model rather than these global geometry shifts.

Direct harmonic-power fit
--------------------------
I tested an objective equal to Ac1^2+As1^2+Ac2^2+As2^2, fitting detector_theta,
detector_phi, detector_tilt_x, and detector_tilt_y together. The result was:
detector_theta = -0.648056 rad
detector_phi = -0.000880 rad
detector_tilt_x = -2.06e-06 rad
detector_tilt_y = 5.31e-04 rad

The resulting coefficients were:
A0  = -0.000391
Ac1 =  0.000117
As1 = -0.000675
Ac2 =  0.000918
As2 =  0.001960

This is not an improvement: the dominant second-harmonic amplitude remains
unchanged or larger. The large parameter uncertainties also show that this
direct harmonic fit is weakly identified and should not be used alone as a
geometry calibration.
